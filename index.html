Translational Biology and Engineering Program

Proteomics for cardiovascular discovery, from bench to bedside.

We measure proteins in cells, tissues and biofluids by mass spectrometry, and work alongside clinicians, academic labs and industry partners to turn those measurements into biomarkers, mechanisms and therapeutic targets.

What proteomics can answer.

How a project runs

From first conversation to interpreted results.

  1. Talk to usTell us the question, the samples and the timeline. We advise on design, replicates and sample handling before anything is collected.
  2. PlanWe agree on scope, methods, costs and, where needed, ethics and data-sharing arrangements.
  3. Prepare & acquireSamples are processed with quality controls and measured by high-resolution LC-MS/MS.
  4. AnalyzeIdentification, quantification, statistics and pathway analysis, done with documented settings.
  5. Report & follow upYou receive an interactive report, full tables and methods text, and we go through the results with you.
