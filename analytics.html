Data & reports

From raw spectra to results you can explore.

Analysis is part of every project. You receive documented, reproducible results, and a report that lets your whole team look at the data without specialist software.

Interactive report

Explore your results, not just read them.

The report is one file that works offline in any browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, so it is suitable for unpublished and confidential data.

A walkthrough of a demo report made from fictitious data. Open the demo report and try it yourself →

What the analysis includes

Four layers, one consistent pipeline.

01

Quality control

Identifications per sample, intensity distributions, missing values, reproducibility and outlier checks, before any biology is interpreted.

02

Differential analysis

Filtering, imputation and statistical testing with multiple-testing correction, for two groups or many, shown as volcano plots and ranked tables.

03

Patterns across samples

Principal component analysis, clustered heatmaps and expression profiles for multi-group and time-course designs.

04

Biological context

Pathway and Gene Ontology enrichment for each comparison, with the option to integrate transcriptomic or clinical data.

Deliverables

What you receive.

Everything needed to understand the results, defend them to reviewers and reuse them later.

  • Interactive report: search, filter and compare in your browser
  • Results workbook: protein, gene and peptide tables with statistics
  • Pathway enrichment tables for every comparison
  • Methods text with all processing parameters
  • Figures that can be saved from the report for presentations and papers
  • A results meeting with the scientist who did the work

Already have proteomics data?

We can analyze or re-analyze existing datasets and return the same interactive report.
