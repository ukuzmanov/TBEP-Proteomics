Collaborate

Science done together.

We work as scientific partners, not a drop-off service. Projects are planned jointly, and we stay involved through interpretation, publication and follow-up studies.

Clinical collaborators

From patient samples to protein evidence.

The Ted Rogers Centre links research with care across its partner hospitals. Proteomics adds a molecular readout to clinical cohorts, biobanks and trials.

We help clinicians and clinician-scientists design studies that will stand up statistically, and we return results in a form that can be combined with clinical data.

What we offer clinical teams

  • Advice on cohort size, matching and controls for proteomic studies
  • Guidance on collection, processing and storage of plasma and tissue
  • Biomarker discovery, followed by targeted verification
  • De-identified sample handling under your approved ethics protocol
  • Results tables structured for linkage to clinical variables
  • Methods text and figures for protocols, grants and papers

Academic collaborators

Mechanism, models and training.

TBEP investigators and trainees come from two University of Toronto faculties and nine departments. We collaborate with labs inside the program, across the University of Toronto and its affiliated hospitals, and at other institutions.

Trainees are welcome at the bench and at the keyboard: learning how the data are made is part of the collaboration.

What we offer academic labs

  • Experimental design consultation before samples are prepared
  • Pilot experiments to establish feasibility
  • Letters of support, methods and budgets for grant applications
  • Co-development of new methods for unusual samples or questions
  • Training for graduate students and postdoctoral fellows
  • Help with data deposition and manuscript preparation

Industry partners

Translational proteomics for companies.

TBEP works with industry partners to move discoveries toward patients. For companies, proteomics can show what a compound does to its target and pathway, support a mechanism of action, or evaluate candidate biomarkers.

Projects can be run as fee-for-service work or as sponsored research collaborations, with agreements arranged through the appropriate institutional offices.

What we offer industry partners

  • A defined scope of work, timeline and deliverables
  • Confidential handling of samples, data and results
  • Target engagement, mechanism-of-action and biomarker studies
  • Access to cardiovascular model systems through TBEP investigators
  • Clear reporting: interactive report, full tables and methods
  • A path from pilot to larger collaborative programs

Working together

What to expect.

Before

A conversation first

Every project starts with a discussion of the question, the samples and what a useful answer would look like.

During

Transparent work

You know what is being done and why. Quality-control results are shared, and unexpected findings are raised early.

After

Support past the report

We go through the results with your team, and help with follow-up experiments, figures, methods and reviewer questions.

Authorship, acknowledgement, cost recovery and data ownership are agreed at the planning stage.

Planning a study, a grant or a partnership?

Contact us early. We can help shape the design and provide the supporting documents you need.
